## 5. PROGRAM KEAHLIAN (ada 4 jurusan: RPL, TKJ, DKV, PSPT)
Kurikulum link & match industri; menyiapkan lulusan siap kerja, berwirausaha digital mandiri, dan siap kuliah. Pendidikan 3 tahun (kelas 10, 11, 12).

### 5.1 RPL - Rekayasa Perangkat Lunak (Teknologi Rekayasa & Informatika; Fullstack Web & Mobile / Software Engineering)
- Tujuan: mencetak Web/Mobile App Developer & Fullstack Engineer dengan portofolio aplikasi berstandar industri.
- Kompetensi inti: Web & Mobile Dev (arsitektur modern, RESTful API, Reactive UI, Progressive Web Apps); Cloud & Database (PostgreSQL, MongoDB, Supabase, dasar containerization Docker, Cloud Deploy); Fundamental AI & ML (prompt engineering, integrasi LLM API, pemrosesan data terstruktur); QA & Git Collab (Git workflow GitHub/GitLab, unit testing, dasar CI/CD).
- Tech stack: React.js, Flutter, Python, Node.js, Next.js, Git VCS (Beranda juga menyebut Laravel).
- Prospek karier: Junior Fullstack Dev, Mobile App Programmer, Frontend Engineer, Software QA.
- Lab: Lab Mac & Core i7, dual-monitor workstation, internet gigabit dedicated, local cloud server.
- Sertifikasi: BNSP Rekayasa Perangkat Lunak; Oracle Certified Associate (OCA).
- Output proyek akhir: aplikasi web/mobile siap pakai di Play Store & live production server.

### 5.2 TKJ - Teknik Komputer & Jaringan (MikroTik & Cisco Academy; Infrastruktur & Keamanan Siber)
- Tujuan: menguasai rancang bangun network enterprise, cloud administration, fiber optic, dan cyber defense.
- Kompetensi inti: Network Routing & Switching (VLAN, OSPF, BGP, load balancing, perangkat Cisco & MikroTik); Cyber Security & Firewall (IDS/IPS, enkripsi data, packet sniffing inspection, hardening Linux server); Fiber Optic Infrastructure (penyambungan fusion splicer, pengukuran OTDR, arsitektur FTTH); Cloud Server & DevOps (virtualisasi Proxmox/VMware, Ubuntu Server, dasar cloud hosting AWS).
- Lisensi/sertifikasi: MikroTik MTCNA (MikroTik Certified Network Associate), Cisco CCNA Prep, BNSP Teknisi Madya (tabel perbandingan menulis "BNSP TKJ"), Linux Professional (LPIC).
- Prospek karier: Network Engineer, Systems Administrator, Cyber Security Analyst, FTTH Technician.
- Lab: Server Rack 42U, router MikroTik RB-Series, Cisco Switch Catalyst, Fusion Splicer Fiber Optic.
- Output proyek akhir: topologi jaringan enterprise aman & implementasi fiber distribution node.

### 5.3 DKV - Desain Komunikasi Visual (Industri Kreatif & Game; UI/UX & 3D Animation; juga disebut Multimedia/DKV)
- Tujuan: visual storytelling - UI/UX product design, 3D asset creation, motion graphics & video sinematik.
- Kompetensi inti: UI/UX & Product Design (user journey mapping, wireframing, prototyping high-fidelity, design system di Figma); 3D Modeling & Animation (Blender 3D: modeling, texturing, rigging, rendering untuk game asset); Motion Graphic & VFX (After Effects, kinetic typography); Sinematografi & Post-Pro (pencahayaan studio, multi-cam, color grading Premiere & DaVinci).
- Software standar industri: Figma, Blender 3D, Adobe Illustrator, Adobe Premiere Pro, After Effects, DaVinci Resolve.
- Prospek karier: UI/UX Designer, Motion Graphic Artist, 3D Modeler, Digital Content Creator.
- Lab: Lab GPU RTX Studio, Pen Display Huion/Wacom, studio foto & podcast green screen multi-cam.
- Sertifikasi: Adobe Certified Professional (ACP).
- Lab DKV di halaman Fasilitas: tempat siswa membuat citra dan animasi digital, mengembangkan laman web interaktif, merekam dan menyunting audio-video (dilengkapi alat pencetak sablon).

### 5.4 PSPT - Produksi & Siaran Program Televisi (Broadcasting & Film; juga ditulis Broadcasting & Perfilman)
- Tujuan: menguasai produksi konten siaran, teknik pengoperasian kamera studio, live broadcasting switcher, audio engineering, dan tata artistik.
- Kompetensi inti: Teknik Kamera & Shooting (kamera studio & lapangan, framing, lighting, multi-camera production); Live Switching & Directing (vision mixer/switcher, live directing, cueing, koordinasi kru siaran langsung); Audio Engineering (mixing console, teknik mikrofon, sound design, audio post-production); Editing & Post Production (Adobe Premiere Pro, DaVinci Resolve, color grading, motion graphic overlay).
- Tools & platform: Live Switcher, Studio Lighting, Multi-Camera, Sound Engineering, Premiere Pro, DaVinci Resolve.
- Prospek karier: Program Director, Camera Operator, Video Editor, Live Streaming Producer, Content Creator TV.
- Lab: Studio TV kedap suara, multi-camera setup 4K, lighting grid, video switcher console & audio mixer, teleprompter room.
- Sertifikasi: BNSP Penyiaran dan Penataan Kamera Televisi.
- Lab PSPT di halaman Fasilitas: siswa berlatih penyutradaraan, kameramen, penyuntingan, penataan artistik dan suara, penulisan naskah (dilengkapi kamera HD).
